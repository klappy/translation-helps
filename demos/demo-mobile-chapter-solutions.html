Recommendation: Solution 1 + Smart Scrolling

Integrate chapter selection into the existing breadcrumb with these enhancements:

  • Chapter selector becomes a dropdown in the breadcrumb (zero additional space)
  • Hide breadcrumbs on scroll down, show on scroll up
  • Optional: Add subtle swipe gestures for power users
  • Long-press on scripture text shows chapter jump menu

This approach adds zero pixels to the mobile UI while maintaining full chapter navigation functionality.
