Translation Notes for Genesis 1:1

This verse introduces the beginning of God's creation of everything that exists. The Hebrew word "בְּרֵאשִׁית" (bərēšîṯ) means "in the beginning" and emphasizes that this is the absolute beginning of time and creation.
