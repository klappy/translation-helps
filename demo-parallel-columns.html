Translation Notes for Genesis 1:1

This verse introduces the creation account. The Hebrew בְּרֵאשִׁית (bərēšîṯ) emphasizes absolute beginning - not just the start of one thing, but the very beginning of time, space, and matter itself.

Key Translation Decisions:

  • בְּרֵאשִׁית - "In the beginning" vs "When God began to create"
  • בָּרָא - Indicates creation ex nihilo (from nothing)
  • הַשָּׁמַיִם וְאֵת הָאָרֶץ - "heavens and earth" = totality of creation
