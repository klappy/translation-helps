Recommendation: Design 1 - Horizontal Scrolling Pills

The horizontal scrolling design provides the best balance of accessibility, usability, and visual appeal. It maintains the clean ETEN Lab aesthetic while offering single-click access to any chapter. The design is touch-friendly, keyboard accessible, and works well on all screen sizes.
